# 🚗 Intelligent Supply Chain Management Platform for Electric Vehicle Manufacturing

## 📖 Project Overview

The Intelligent Supply Chain Management Platform for Electric Vehicle Manufacturing is an AI-powered decision intelligence system designed to help EV manufacturers optimize supply chain operations. The platform leverages Agentic AI, Graph-RAG, Machine Learning, and Knowledge Graph technology to forecast demand, optimize inventory, analyze supplier risks, and generate procurement recommendations.

---

## ❗ Problem Statement

Electric Vehicle (EV) manufacturers face challenges such as demand fluctuations, inventory shortages, supplier delays, and procurement inefficiencies. Existing systems are reactive and require manual analysis of large amounts of data, leading to production disruptions, delayed deliveries, and increased operational costs.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Forecast EV demand and battery requirements using AI-driven predictive analytics.
- Optimize inventory levels to prevent shortages and overstocking.
- Analyze supplier performance and predict potential supply chain risks.
- Build a Knowledge Graph to model supply chain dependencies and relationships.
- Develop a Graph-RAG assistant for intelligent document retrieval and question answering.
- Implement multiple AI agents for autonomous supply chain decision support.
- Generate procurement and inventory recommendations.
- Provide real-time supply chain insights through interactive dashboards.
